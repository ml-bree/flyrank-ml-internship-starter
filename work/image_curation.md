## 2. Image Decisions

### Home Hero: No Image
"I'm keeping the hero section clean with just my claim and tagline over whitespace. The work itself — shown in the screenshots below — is where the visual interest lives. An AI-generated hero would compete with the real proof."

### Work Screenshots: Real Captures
"Using real, unedited screenshots of my FlyRank notebook outputs and charts. This is the core evidence that my ML pipeline works — a generated image couldn't prove that. A clean, cropped capture of the Precision@50 chart tells the story immediately."

### About Photo: Real Photo
"I'll use a clear, professional photo of myself. A generated avatar would feel impersonal for an 'About' section."

### Project Icons: Not Used (or Generated Consistently)
"I'm not using decorative project icons on the work page. The screenshots themselves provide the necessary visuals. If needed, I would generate a simple line-art icon in a single accent color (#6B8F71), but for now, text and proof are enough."

## 3. Rejection Note (The Discernment)

I generated a dark, glowing neural-network-style hero image for the homepage, but I rejected it immediately. It had the 'AI slop' look — fake glass, generic nodes, and it looked like a stock image. Worse, it would have competed with my real screenshots. A clean title over whitespace lets the actual work — my precision chart — be the first thing someone remembers.

I also generated a futuristic 'AI Brain' icon for the project card, but I rejected it because it was too flashy and didn't match the calm, professional mood of my identity kit. I decided to use no icon for now and let the text and numbers speak. The skill is rejecting the flashy thing that gets in the way of the real thing.
