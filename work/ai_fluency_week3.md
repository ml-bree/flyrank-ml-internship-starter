## Identity Kit

**Fonts:** Inter (heading and body)

**Palette:**
- Main: #6B8F71 (moss green)
- Text: #14161A (near-black)
- Background: #F5F3F0 (warm white)
- Accent: #C9714F (clay)

**Logo/Favicon:** BR monogram in moss green

**Style Note:**
Clean and calm. Inter font, warm-white background (#F5F3F0), near-black text (#14161A), moss-green accent (#6B8F71). The work is the loudest thing on the page.

### Rejection Note

I generated a dark, glowing neural-network-style hero image for the homepage, but I rejected it immediately. It had the 'AI slop' look — fake glass, generic nodes, and it looked like a stock image. Worse, it would have competed with my real screenshots. A clean title over whitespace lets the actual work — my precision chart — be the first thing someone remembers.

I also generated a futuristic 'AI Brain' icon for the project card, but I rejected it because it was too flashy and didn't match the calm, professional mood of my identity kit. I decided to use no icon for now and let the text and numbers speak. The skill is rejecting the flashy thing that gets in the way of the real thing.
